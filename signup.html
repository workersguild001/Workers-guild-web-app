The Workers Guild - Terms and Conditions

Welcome to The Workers Guild. By enlisting, you agree to the following protocols:

  1. Verification Protocol: All operatives must provide valid identification and proof of trade skills. Misrepresentation of expertise will result in immediate expulsion from the guild.
  2. Workmanship Standard: Every job is tracked. Consistently high-quality work is rewarded with XP; substandard work will impact your guild standing and access to premium job postings.
  3. Confidentiality: Client data and job site specifics are strictly confidential. Sharing internal guild communications or trade secrets with external entities is prohibited.
  4. Safety Compliance: You agree to adhere to all safety regulations relevant to your trade. The Guild provides tracking and verification, but operational safety remains the responsibility of the operative.
  5. Mutual Respect: The Guild is built by workers, for workers. Professionalism and respect between operatives and clients are non-negotiable.

Failure to adhere to these protocols may result in the suspension of your credentials. Thank you for building the future with us.
